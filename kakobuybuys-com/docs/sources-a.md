## Content safeguards
- No invented fees, coupon amounts, storage periods, transit guarantees, product counts, reviews or personal purchase experiences.
- Site identified as independent.
- No source brand appears in user-facing article or FAQ prose.
- Seller product photos and verified QC are distinguished.
- Historical QC is not presented as evidence for the visitor's own received item.
- Image search and QC feeds are not described as implemented without source capability validation.
- URLs in sources are editorial source metadata; the site owner decides how references appear in the UI.
- Includes practical original recommendations and hypotheticals, not official promises.
