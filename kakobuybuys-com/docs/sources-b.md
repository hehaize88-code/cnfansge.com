# Kakobuy research notes for five English articles

Research and editorial review: 2026-10-24 (Asia/Shanghai).
Output: content-b.json, five articles, each 1,200–1,600 words, ten sections each.

## Source method

Searched current official Kakobuy help and tutorial pages, then reviewed returned indexed article text. Some direct page opens are JavaScript shells; do not describe all policies as having been checked inside a logged-in account. No account access, purchase, payment or private-order inspection occurred. Official snippets/full indexed extracts are evidence for narrow platform facts. All checklists, comparisons and hypothetical arithmetic are original editorial guidance, not copied policy prose.

## Official evidence and boundaries

- https://kakobuy.com/enquiry
  Describes buying from different sellers, warehouse receipt, checking and choosing stored products for one parcel. Supports consolidation as a workflow. Does not prove a guaranteed saving or any current fee.
- https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=1
  Storage rules with status-dependent start points and disposal consequences. The article deliberately omits fixed day counts and directs readers to their current applicable deadline.
- https://kakobuy.com/service/help/question?cid=35&id=84
  Arrival, inspection/measurement, eligibility to submit a waybill, and possible differences after final packing. Supports separating arrived from ready and estimates from final freight.
- https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=95
  Inspection/warehouse information and limits of inspection. The articles do not equate QC photographs with authenticity or full functional testing.
- https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=37
  Route dimension issues, potential route changes and address/contact requirements. Used for checking the actual route and correct delivery details; no country-specific legal claims.
- https://item.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=87
  Cancellation and return handling depend on order stage; seller consent and return freight can matter; certain refunds go to account balance. Avoid treating the article's legacy windows/times as universal promises.
- https://item.kakobuy.com/index/information?information_id=10
  Return/exchange condition and completeness limits. This page's fee wording does not fully align with all other help material; no universal free-return promise is repeated.
- https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=51
  Return/exchange page with specific conditions. Cross-checked as an official source but exact windows/charges are not reproduced because applicability needs current order confirmation.
- https://www.kakobuy.com/index/information?information_id=13
  Official customer-service route. No operating hours guaranteed in the articles.
- https://www.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?cid=63&id=40
  General service terms, accurate recipient information, account responsibility and after-sales reference. No legal advice or fixed customs rules are inferred.
- https://kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=74
  Cost components: goods, domestic shipping, international shipping, packaging and optional services. Used for separating budgets, not quoting a total.
- https://item.kakobuy.com/service/help/question?id=99
  Payment guidance says handling charge is subject to the actual payment. Indexed text also contains conflicting legacy material: says four methods but lists three in its first section, includes stray SUGARGOO wording, a fixed old PayPal fee and unfinished bank fields. Articles explicitly avoid publishing those numbers or implying all listed methods are available. Never reuse bank details from this page.
- https://www.kakobuy.com/tools/estimate
  Official shipping estimator reference; actual route and payable checkout must be inspected.
- https://item2.kakobuy.com/index/information?information_id=3

  Alternate official tutorial URL, used in the links guide.

## Keyword and competitor observations

https://kakobuyagent.com/kakobuy-order-guide covers order status, warehouse arrivals and cancellation questions; useful keyword themes are “kakobuy order process”, “kakobuy warehouse” and “kakobuy first haul”.
https://kakobuyspreadsheetplus.com/fees-refunds combines total-cost planning and claims; useful themes are “kakobuy fees”, “kakobuy refunds” and “kakobuy payment fees”.
https://kakobuyservices.com/kakobuy-services groups inspection, storage, parcel preparation and support; useful themes are “kakobuy consolidation” and “kakobuy shipping checklist”.
Competitors were used only to identify questions and content organization. Their timings, fees, statistics, guarantees and sample products were not adopted. No search volume or keyword-difficulty data was obtained.

## Editorial choices

- All five articles are independent explanatory content; no invented reviews, traffic totals, guaranteed savings or invented coupon offers.
- The only numeric payment example is explicitly hypothetical arithmetic, not a quote.
- No counterfeit authentication claims, customs evasion advice or fixed tax thresholds.
- No claim that this preview already has working image-search or QC-photo interfaces.
- Product-source branding is omitted from user-facing copy, as requested. No standalone merchandise database is proposed.
- The articles distinguish account-balance refunds from repayments/withdrawals and preserve seller/agent responsibilities.
- Sources arrays are for editorial provenance. If rendered as visible links, avoid displaying other affiliates' item-link examples as buying buttons.
