## Important uncertainties and conflicting official wording

- **Storage:** No verified free-storage duration, storage fee schedule or disposal deadline was found. Do not invent 30/90/180-day claims. Send the reader to their order/warehouse account or official support.
- **Return fees:** R's eligibility table and fee section differ on recovering the original seller-to-warehouse postage for initially free-shipping items. The fee section lists RMB 5 service charge plus transport. U also mentions one free eligible return/exchange per 30 days. Avoid a universal price or unlimited-free-returns promise; direct readers to the current order quote.
- **Shipping platform fee:** U explicitly lists **8% of the shipping fee**, subject to settlement/exchange differences. This is evidence against describing the entire service as universally free. A simple FAQ should say to review the current fee breakdown, with U linked, rather than calculate a universal total itself.
- **QC limits:** T's broad professional-inspection language is qualified by U and F. Do not equate photos with authenticity certification, laboratory testing, or flawless goods.
- **Restricted goods:** A's broad marketplace-access marketing does not override T's prohibition of infringing/counterfeit goods. Avoid copying competitor replica endorsements into guides.
- **Payment methods:** Official footer icons include Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Klarna, Google Pay and Apple Pay. This is not confirmation of regional/account availability, fees, refunds or transaction eligibility.
- **Tracking:** G's three-day statement concerns first tracking availability after dispatch; it does not promise receipt in three days or a fixed international transit duration.
- **Support email:** Search rendering obfuscated its address. Link the contact page instead of guessing or copying an unrelated address.
- **Legal wording:** T contains differing governing-law clauses. The site should not supply its own interpretation of jurisdiction.
- **Tax, insurance and delivery:** No universal tax-free guarantee, guaranteed delivery window, automatic full compensation, or universal return approval is supported.
