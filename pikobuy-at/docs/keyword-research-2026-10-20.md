# Pikobuy keyword and competitor research

Research date: 20 October 2026. Scope: English-language discovery for pikobuy.at, with `pikobuy spreadsheet` as the homepage's main keyword and ten practical buyer-help articles. This is a qualitative review of returned search results and inspected public pages. Search volumes, ranking positions, difficulty scores, traffic, and conversion rates were not available and are not claimed. Returned pages show competing vocabulary and coverage, not measured demand.

## Quality opportunities and boundaries

These are opportunities to provide more actionable material within the inspected sample, not proven globally unserved keywords:

- Connect each instruction to the feature that actually exists on pikobuy.at and cnfansge.com. Broad competitor guides often discuss a workflow without making the specific source/data boundary immediately clear.
- Make the buyer's next action explicit: which link to open, which measurement to record, which photo to inspect, which evidence to send. Each article should contain an original checklist, table or short worked method, not just reordered FAQ paragraphs.
- Separate three decisions that often blur together: estimating international shipping, budgeting the full purchase, and deciding how to pack the parcel.
- Distinguish historical QC from the buyer's own order QC in the article, FAQ and product interface. Absence of QC should be explained honestly.
- Keep policy facts attached to exact official source URLs and a real checked date. Do not infer a promise from a competitor's promotional heading.
- Offer a short answer first, readable sections, two to four contextual internal links, and a compact official-sources block. An article's length should follow its subject; do not pad to a word target.
- Avoid evergreen titles with “2026” unless meaningful date-sensitive content is regularly rechecked. Do not claim “best,” verified sellers, stock, authenticity, shipping discounts, free services or specific delivery days without applicable evidence.

## Keyword ownership and links

- Homepage: `pikobuy spreadsheet`; supporting natural variants `Pikobuy finds`, `Pikobuy product links`.
- Directory/search pages: product and category discovery queries only where cnfansge provides real records; avoid thin indexable empty search results.
- FAQ: direct short answers for common questions, with links to the deeper relevant article. FAQ expansion should not duplicate all article prose.
- Articles index: Pikobuy guides and buying help; group by Find, Order, Inspect, Ship, Resolve rather than ten nearly identical cards.
- Each article links to one principal action (browse real products, open the official estimator, register, or use official support) and two or three closely related guides. Do not point all anchors to the homepage.
- The `.at` domain alone does not establish the audience language. English is supported by this research. Translate only complete, reviewed pages; retain language-specific titles, self-canonicals and correct reciprocal hreflang rather than indexing untranslated copies.
