## Presentation and measurement

Homepage and article counts are derived from the actual article data. New guides appear on the homepage and within existing topic groups. Core navigation remains available. Article contents use a compact disclosure; full paragraphs remain visible. Mobile article-card minimum heights and excess spacing are reduced without shortening content.

Existing GA4 tag `G-K4JW68ZWVN` and existing events retained. Guide clicks now distinguish next-step and card placements. Added `resource_click` for official source links and `article_end_view` when the conclusion enters the viewport. The latter is an exposure signal, not proof that an article was read. No search text, credentials or personal details are sent by these additions. Event reporting should be checked after real user traffic; static validation cannot establish GA4 ingestion.
