# Sugargoos.uk manual SEO release — 15 October 2026

## Evidence and scope

The owner requested research, improvements, publication and complete language
coverage. This release uses the existing GitHub `main` → Pages build integration.
It does not resume the paused article automation or change hosting configuration.

Priorities combine first-party analysis with public competitor content and gaps
in the existing site. Private dashboard metrics and account identifiers are not
included in this public repository. No Bing performance or keyword-volume claim
is made. The existing public GA4 tag is preserved.

Public examples reviewed included Jadeship's Sugargoo spreadsheet search and
sugargo.co.uk's UK shipping guide. They show competing search/filter/QC and
shipping content; they do not establish competitor traffic, conversion rates or
ranking in a fixed UK search location. Priorities reflect task intent, not
invented search volumes. Technical indexability does not guarantee indexing.

## Changes

- Four new full-length articles in each of EN/DE/ES/FR/IT: 20 new localized
  pages, bringing the total to 85 article pages and 155 localized routes.
- Shipping-cost, tracking, QC-photo and volumetric-weight articles receive
  localized intent-led titles, complete descriptions, concise decision summaries
  and usable tables. Cost and rehearsal pages include the weight calculator.
- Homepage displays four new articles and direct links to spreadsheet, cost, QC
  and payment tasks. The complete article hub groups all 17 articles by task.
- Related-article links connect new guides to relevant existing articles and hubs.
  Article titles are clickable; all articles gain a back-to-hub link.
- Preserved self-canonicals and five-language HTML hreflang, with reciprocal
  alternatives and x-default added to the generated sitemap.
- New custom GA4 events: `search_submit`, `product_click`, `category_click`,
  `guide_click`, `calculator_use`. Search events send query length only, never
  the user's raw query. These events are not automatically marked as key events.
  Existing GA4 enhanced-measurement `click` events may also exist; do not sum them
  with custom events as unique interactions.

## Verification and follow-up

Build with `npm run build` (the Sites build wrapper invokes this same script).
Run `node scripts/verify-static.mjs` on the output before publishing. This checks
all 155 routes, article counts, local links, metadata, language alternatives,
structured article data, generated sitemap and interaction-script inclusion.

The managed environment does not provide the required local browser-control
capability, so local browser rendering QA is unavailable. Static export and HTML
checks remain available; production content must be confirmed after deployment.

After search engines have recrawled, compare complete 28-day windows. First check
index coverage and non-brand impressions; then query/page CTR at comparable
positions and country/device mix. In GA4, segment Organic Search and landing page,
then examine guide and product interactions. Do not interpret a small number of events as organic growth. GA4 ingestion, consent,
ad blockers and browser settings can affect recorded counts. No ranking or traffic
increase is guaranteed by this release.
