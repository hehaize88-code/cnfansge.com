## Data limitations

Live product listing images are not equivalent to order-specific warehouse QC photos. No verified public cnfansge image-search or independent QC lookup endpoint has been established. The preview states this explicitly and does not accept image uploads or claim inspection results. All actual product records, images, search results, sort ordering and pagination come from cnfansge.com.
