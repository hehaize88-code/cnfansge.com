Cost model

Item cost + domestic delivery + international shipping + tax & fees

The cheapest product does not always produce the cheapest delivered parcel. Bulky packaging, restricted goods and low-value lines can materially change the final total.

01

Understand chargeable weight

Carriers may compare actual weight with volumetric weight and charge whichever their line rules specify.

Why a light parcel can still cost more

Large boxes occupy aircraft or vehicle space even when the contents are light. Ask whether packaging can be removed safely, but consider whether the retail box provides protection or resale value.

1

Consolidate intentionally

The official Hipobuy app listing states 90 days of free storage. Use the window to compare one-parcel and split-parcel plans; a larger box can increase dimensional weight or remove a preferred route.

2

Compare eligible lines

Compare only the routes actually shown for your destination, category, weight and dimensions.

3

Review declaration rules

Use accurate information and follow destination laws. This guide does not recommend undervaluing or misdescribing a parcel.

4

Plan for tax and customs

Import tax, VAT, duty and clearance procedures depend on the destination and shipment. Verify official local guidance before paying.

5

Record the parcel

Save the parcel number, line, declared information, packed weight, tracking number and insurance terms for later reference.

02

Compare the quote like an invoice

Current user reviews repeatedly show why shipping should be estimated before the warehouse stage.

Write these fields side by side

  • Final actual weight and package dimensions
  • Chargeable weight and billing rule used by each line
  • Base quote, packing services and insurance
  • Restrictions, tracking and compensation limits
  • Estimated delivery range and last-mile carrier where shown
  • Destination tax or customs treatment stated for the route
