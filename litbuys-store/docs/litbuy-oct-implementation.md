## Editorial boundaries

Public competitor pages informed category and task coverage; they do not establish
search volumes. Priority is an editorial inference from query intent and the
site's existing coverage. No ranking, click growth or indexing outcome is promised.
Exact platform ratings, delivery times and storage periods are not promoted as
freshly verified current promises. Historical descriptions are identified, and
readers are directed to current account and route terms.

The source notes in this directory document public evidence and limitations.
Private search and analytics report details are deliberately not published here.
