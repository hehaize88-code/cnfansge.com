# LitBuy coupon guide — editorial record

Prepared 7 November 2026. Content: `app/content-nov/coupons.json`, slug `litbuy-coupons-guide`.

## Research basis

The parent audit checked official public search results for `https://m.litbuy.com/`, `https://litbuy.com/pages/` and `https://litbuy.com/help`. The first two display shipping coupons, including minimum-freight conditions and percentage offers. Promotional banners and amounts differ across indexed pages; the article intentionally does not claim a particular code or discount is currently available to every reader. No authenticated checkout or customer payment was tested.

Only the existence and freight-specific nature of public coupon offers inform factual platform descriptions. The eligibility checklist, troubleshooting sequence, comparison method and support-record suggestions are original general guidance. They do not assert that every listed restriction applies to every LitBuy coupon. Account terms and the final payment breakdown determine actual eligibility, permitted combinations and cancellation treatment.

## Original examples

- Fictional payment: 100 eligible units + 20 excluded = 120. A hypothetical 20% discount capped at 15 gives a total of 105. A separate fixed 18-unit offer, if fully applicable, gives 102. These are not LitBuy rates or active offers.
- Fictional route comparison: 110 minus 10 = 100, compared with a suitable 90-unit route without a coupon.
- Adding 18 units of unwanted products to save another 5 costs at least 13 extra before shipping changes.
