## Editorial decisions

- Shipping owns the complete cost and handover workflow: purchase versus international payments; domestic transport; optional services; comparable route quotes; packing; destination charges; tracking; delivery evidence. It does not replace the narrower weight guide.
- Shipping calculation: 40 × 30 × 25 cm = 30,000 cm³; illustrative divisors 6,000 and 5,000 produce 5 kg and 6 kg. Every language explicitly says these are examples and that the selected route determines its actual divisor, rounding and chargeable-weight rules.
- Destination discussion gives no fixed US duty-free threshold, tax rate, tax-exemption promise or declaration manipulation advice. It asks readers to consult current destination and selected-route conditions.
- Review is an independent evaluation framework, explicitly not a firsthand purchase test. Seller, purchasing service, warehouse, carrier and destination authority are separated without purporting to determine contractual liability.
- Historical claims about Trustpilot, removed fake reviews, precise app ratings and fixed storage durations are removed. Reviews are discussed as contextual evidence, not a statistical success rate.
- No Q&A sections, invented testimonials, artificial ratings, third-party merchant links or product recommendations without evidence are included. Content has no URLs; the parent implementation owns internal and approved main-site links.
- All five languages contain the same eight sections and two complete paragraphs in each section. No language falls back to English. Translations preserve the distinction between original guidance, an illustration and a verified fact.
