## Editorial decisions

- `allchinabuy-shipping-cost-guide` targets shipping cost, parcel budget and shipping-calculator intent. It is an original budgeting workflow, not a rate list. Fee categories are conditional record fields rather than asserted AllChinaBuy charges.
- `actual-weight-vs-volumetric-weight` targets actual weight, volumetric weight and packed dimensions. It teaches units and reproducible calculations. Hypothetical box: 40 × 30 × 25 cm = 30,000 cm³; D = 6,000 gives 5 kg, D = 5,000 gives 6 kg. Both are expressly fictional service rules. Alternative 40 × 30 × 20 cm = 24,000 cm³; hypothetical D = 6,000 gives 4 kg. No monetary saving is promised.
- No customs thresholds, legal advice, fixed delivery promises, live shipping quotes, claims of checkout testing or invented platform policy.
- cnfansge remains the sole product data source. Article wording distinguishes product records from warehouse measurements and does not claim this child site operates an official shipping calculator.
- Sources are linked as research credits. No copied passages, charts, photographs, product database or backend were created.
- All five versions preserve the same two slugs, 12 sections per article and 2 paragraphs per section, including uncertainty, numerical examples and limits. Translations are full editorial translations, not short summaries.
